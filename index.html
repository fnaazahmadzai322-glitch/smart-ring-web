OUR STORY

Built at the intersection
of craft and code.

NOVA Ring began in Dubai in 2023, when a small team of biomedical engineers and goldsmiths asked a simple question: why does wearable technology have to look like technology? Everything on the market felt like a gadget strapped to your body — plastic, blinking, temporary. We wanted something you'd still want to wear after the battery ran out.

So we rebuilt the smart ring from the inside out. Medical-grade sensors and a custom low-power chip went in first; the sterling silver and 18k gold PVD shell came after, shaped and polished by hand in the same ateliers that finish fine jewelry. The result reads your heart rate, sleep, and stress around the clock — and reads, to everyone else, as simply a beautiful ring.

Today NOVA Ring is worn across the UAE and shipped throughout the GCC, from boardrooms in DIFC to early runs along Kite Beach. Our promise hasn't changed since day one: quiet intelligence, unmistakable design.
